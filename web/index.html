Gokartcentralen Göteborg

The indoor rental-kart track from Formula Driverless Path Predictor, driven in the browser.

  • Steer — left stick (or A / D, arrows)
  • Throttle — right trigger (or W, up arrow)
  • Brake / reverse — left trigger (or S, space)
  • Camera — triangle or circle (or C) · Reset — square (or R)

On a Mac, connect the DualShock 4 by Bluetooth, then press any button on it. Chrome or Edge give the best controller support; Safari works too. The coloured line on the track is the operator's own poster line — green gas, yellow lift, red brake.
