$$ v_{\max} \leq \sqrt{\mu gR} $$

mass cancels.

Do not artificially multiply steering behavior by mass.
Do not imply that storing mass_kg makes the simulation mass-sensitive.

A future dynamic model can make mass, yaw inertia, force limits, and load effects meaningful.

45. Understand the later optimization direction
